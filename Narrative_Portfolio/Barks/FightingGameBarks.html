Shiro Kurose “Afterimage”

Combat dialogue built around speed, misdirection, and a competitive hero-in-training.

Barks My Hero Academia OC Dialogue

Project Description

This assignment is a character bark bank for Shiro Kurose, whose hero name is Afterimage. The goal of the bank is to create short lines of dialogue for different moments during a fighting game match, giving the character a recognizable voice throughout combat. The lines cover reactions to damage, low health, critical hits, guarding, special moves, successful combos, wins, losses, and throws.

Shiro's Quirk, Echo Step, lets her create temporary afterimages that mimic her movements. Her dialogue reflects that ability through references to speed, confusion, and opponents losing track of her. She is energetic, competitive, and a little cocky, but she also takes hero work seriously and believes quick thinking can matter as much as raw strength.

CHARACTER SNAPSHOT

**Hero Name:** Afterimage

Original Character Concept

Shiro is an aspiring Pro Hero who uses temporary copies of herself to confuse opponents and make her attacks difficult to predict. In combat, she relies on speed, misdirection, and carefully timed strikes to overwhelm her opponent. She wants to prove that a hero does not need the most powerful Quirk to make a difference.

LOOKING BACK

Reflection

Looking back on the writing process, the character voice, and how the dialogue supports gameplay.

What I Learned

Through this assignment, I learned how important it is to keep character dialogue short, recognizable, and appropriate to the situation. Fighting games rely on quick interactions, so each line needs to communicate a character's personality without interrupting the flow of combat. I also learned that writing multiple variations for the same trigger helps make dialogue feel less repetitive while still maintaining a consistent character voice. Shiro's dialogue needed to reflect her energetic, competitive personality while incorporating her Quirk, Echo Step, through references to speed, misdirection, and afterimages.

What Worked Well

I think one of the strongest parts of this bark bank is how Shiro's personality comes through in her combat dialogue. Lines like "Which one's the real me?" and "Lost track of me already?" connect directly to her Quirk and fighting style. I also wanted to show that she isn't just competitive or cocky, but genuinely motivated to become a Pro Hero. Her victory line, "Another step toward being top hero!" helps communicate that ambition. I think having both playful and more serious reactions makes her feel like a more believable character.

What I Would Develop Further

If I continued developing this bark bank, I would like to expand the dialogue with more opponent-specific interactions and additional reactions to different combat situations. For example, I could write unique dialogue for facing certain characters, recovering from a difficult fight, or using specific combinations of moves. I would also like to explore how Shiro's tone changes depending on the situation, especially when she is losing or facing a stronger opponent. This would give her more personality beyond her initial energetic and competitive attitude.
