PROJECT DESCRIPTION

WHY I MADE THIS

Giving a character a voice in the middle of the action.

For this assignment, I created a character bark bank for Yuna Mori, an original Sentinel agent known by the callsign Pulse. The bank organizes short pieces of in-game dialogue by context and trigger, including match starts, ability use, kills, kill streaks, taunts, idle moments, and the Spike spawning.

Pulse is a technology specialist who manipulates communication signals and gathers battlefield information. Her personality is clever, sarcastic, observant, and confident, so I used her dialogue to communicate both her technical expertise and her attitude. The lines are designed to respond to gameplay moments while helping the player understand who she is.

I also wrote variations for many triggers and character-specific interactions with agents such as Killjoy, Jett, Cypher, Sova, Raze, Omen, Phoenix, and Sage. These exchanges help show how Pulse's voice changes depending on whether she is addressing an ally or an opponent.

01 / AT A GLANCE

Information is her strongest weapon.

Yuna Mori, or Pulse, is a Sentinel recruited by the Valorant Protocol for her ability to manipulate communication signals and collect information about enemy movement. Before joining the Protocol, she worked on experimental communication systems and developed gadgets for detection and signal disruption.

In a team, Pulse focuses on protecting areas, gathering information, and helping her allies prepare for enemy attacks. Her confidence comes through in quick observations, dry humor, and playful challenges.

Confident, quick, and a little cutting

Pulse's dialogue balances tactical awareness with a sarcastic sense of humor. She often frames combat through technology and signals, making her lines feel connected to her specialty rather than interchangeable with another agent's voice.

04 / DIALOGUE STRUCTURE

Writing for gameplay moments

The bark bank is organized around the moments when a player might hear Pulse speak. Many triggers include alternate lines, giving repeated gameplay events more than one possible response.

01

Combat and abilities

Match-start lines establish her confidence. Ability barks for Tracepoint Sensor, Dead Air, Feedback, and her ultimate Blackout connect the dialogue directly to her equipment and signal-based powers.

02

Kills, streaks, and taunts

Short reactions celebrate successful plays and let Pulse tease opponents. Her taunts often use technical language, such as questioning an enemy's aim or suggesting that their settings are the problem.

03

Ally and enemy interactions

Character-specific barks reveal relationships through banter. With Killjoy, Pulse turns their shared interest in inventions into friendly competition. With Cypher, she jokes about cameras and information gathering; with Sova, she recognizes how their tracking skills could work together.

04

Idle and objective moments

Between rounds, Pulse comments on taking apart equipment or borrowing a tool. When the Spike spawns, she shifts back toward the objective and reminds the team to retrieve it.

05 / CHARACTER DYNAMICS

Relationships through dialogue

KILLJOY / FRIENDLY RIVAL

Two inventors, one competition

Pulse gets along with Killjoy because they share an interest in technology, but their conversations sometimes become a competition over whose inventions are better. Pulse's lines tease Killjoy while still showing respect for her abilities.

Tone: Competitive, teasing, familiar, and good-natured.

CYPHER / MUTUAL CURIOSITY

Information has its limits

Pulse finds Cypher's methods interesting, but she does not completely trust him. Her lines challenge his reliance on cameras and spying, while also acknowledging that they share an interest in collecting information.

Tone: Curious, skeptical, and playfully suspicious.

SOVA / TACTICAL PARTNERSHIP

Find them, then track them

Pulse recognizes the value of Sova's recon skills and imagines how their abilities could complement one another. Their dialogue has a more cooperative feel, even when she lightly teases him.

Tone: Respectful, collaborative, lightly teasing.

OTHER AGENTS

A distinct response for each teammate

Her barks with Jett, Raze, Omen, Phoenix, and Sage give each interaction its own angle, from jokes about speed and explosions to startled reactions and appreciation for team support.

Tone: Changes with the character and situation.
