CHARACTER DESIGN DOCUMENT

Arreguii Awa // Guii

A devoted guardian learning that loyalty and choice do not have to be opposites.

Character Design Genshin Impact OC Worldbuilding

Why I made this

Project Description

For this assignment, I created a character design document for Arreguii Awa (Guii), an original character set in the world of Genshin Impact. I used the document to bring together the details that would guide how she is written and understood, her role in the story, motivations, ancestry, voice, relationships, and personal boundaries.

I wanted to create a character who could be both an intimidating and disciplined guardian and someone who is awkward, excitable, and easily frightened outside of her duties. Her loyalty to the Tsaritsa gives her a clear purpose, while her growing connection with the Traveler creates the opportunity for her to question what loyalty means to her.

My goal was to make Guii feel consistent and believable across different situations, while leaving room for her identity and relationships to develop throughout the story.

AT A GLANCE

A guardian caught between duty and identity.

Arreguii Awa, usually called Guii, is a high-ranking guard from Snezhnaya assigned to protect the Tsaritsa. When the Traveler arrives and begins to conflict with Snezhnaya's interests, Guii is forced to reconsider where her loyalty truly belongs.

She appears elegant, otherworldly, and intimidating at first. Beneath her formal exterior, however, she is approachable, excitable, awkward, and easily frightened. Her ancient Hydro Dragon ancestry gives her crystalline horns, wings, and a tail, while her Cryo Vision and Snezhnayan upbringing shape the identity she has chosen for herself.

CHARACTER MOTIVATION

Want vs. Need

WANT

To protect the Tsaritsa.

Guii wants to faithfully protect the Tsaritsa and fulfill her duty to Snezhnaya. She considers her position both an honor and a responsibility. When the Traveler arrives, she initially sees them as a potential threat and is ordered to observe their movements and prevent interference with the Tsaritsa's plans.

Repeated encounters gradually complicate her understanding of the situation. She begins questioning whether following an order is always the same thing as doing what is right.

NEED

To choose what loyalty means.

Guii needs to understand that loyalty does not mean surrendering her ability to choose. She has always defined herself through her responsibilities: guard, protector, Snezhnayan, and descendant of an ancient dragon bloodline.

She finds comfort in knowing exactly what she is supposed to do. Ultimately, she must decide whether her loyalty comes from genuine belief or simply from fear of failing the role she was given.

HISTORY & WORLD

Backstory that matters

01

Hydro Dragon ancestry

Guii's family descends from an ancient Hydro Dragon bloodline associated with Fontaine. Generations ago, members of the family traveled north and settled in Snezhnaya. Guii inherited crystalline horns, wings, and a dragon-like tail from this ancestry.

02

A connection to the Seelies

Her family has a mysterious connection to the Seelies. They have been unusually drawn to members of Guii's bloodline for generations, although the reason has been lost. Guii's own connection appears particularly strong.

03

A Cryo Vision

Guii received a Cryo Vision despite her Hydro Dragon heritage. Rather than being ashamed of this contradiction, she eventually came to view it as proof that she could forge an identity separate from her ancestry.

DIALOGUE & PERFORMANCE

Voice model

Guii speaks politely and carefully while performing her duties. Around authority figures, she becomes especially formal and controlled. With people she trusts, she is more casual, talks quickly when excited, and tends to ramble when nervous. When frightened, her composure can collapse in seconds. When genuinely angry, she becomes quieter and more direct rather than louder.

CHARACTER DYNAMICS

Relationships

ALLY / THE TRAVELER

From suspicion to trust

Guii initially sees the Traveler as an obstacle to Snezhnaya's interests. Her orders require her to monitor their actions and, if necessary, prevent them from reaching the Tsaritsa. Their repeated encounters gradually change her perspective. She begins challenging her assumptions about loyalty and morality, and eventually becomes willing to cooperate when she realizes their goals may overlap.

Tone: Formal and suspicious at first; gradually teasing, frustrated, and eventually genuinely trusting.

ANTAGONIST / A FATUI RESEARCHER

Seen as a subject, not a person

A Fatui researcher becomes interested in Guii's unusual bloodline and Seelie connection. Rather than viewing her as a person, the researcher sees an opportunity to study rare elemental and draconic traits. Guii initially cooperates, hoping to learn about her ancestry, but eventually realizes the researcher is willing to manipulate or endanger her for knowledge.

Tone: Cautiously polite, then suspicious, and eventually unusually firm and angry.

WRITING GUIDELINES

Do / Don't list

DO
  • Protect the person she has been assigned to guard, even when she disagrees with them.
  • Put herself in danger before deliberately putting an innocent person in danger.
  • Follow through once she gives someone her word. Promises are a matter of personal honor.
DON'T
  • Treat someone as lesser because they are human. Guii does not believe her heritage makes her superior.
  • Allow someone to define her solely by her bloodline. She wants the freedom to decide who she is.
  • Abandon someone she has genuinely grown attached to simply to pursue her own goals.
